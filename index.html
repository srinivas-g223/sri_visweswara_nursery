About Sri Visweswara Nursery

Healthy plants from the heart of Kadiam’s nursery belt.

We are a trusted nursery in Kadiyapulanka, part of the Kadiam area near Rajahmundry, a region well known across India for its nurseries and flower festivals. We serve Kadiam and East Godavari with healthy plants, a wide variety, gardening solutions and landscaping support, with service that starts with listening.

We grow and supply flowering, ornamental, indoor, outdoor and fruit plants, along with palms, hedges and landscaping stock. Whether you want one money plant for your desk or plants for a whole compound, we help you choose what will actually grow well in your space.
